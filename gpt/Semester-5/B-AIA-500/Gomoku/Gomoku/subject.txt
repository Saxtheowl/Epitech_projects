GAME RULES

This is a 2-player game that is played on a 20x20 game board (called goban). We use a very simplified
version of the game. Every player plays a stone at his/her turn, and the game ends as soon as one has a 5
stones in a row (vertically, horizontally or diagonaly) - and thus wins.

Watch out! The goban size on Piskvork is 20x20 (and not 19x19) by default. . .

GRAND TOURNAMENT

Your bot will be evaluated based on its results in actual game playing, via a 3-step process:

• play to win

In order to validate the module, your program is intended to understand some winning situations and
play the right move. We will send you pre-filled board and your programm must make the right move,
for example if there is a winnig move your program must play it.

• outsmart local bots

Programs are matched against bots of low to medium levels. The aim is to maximize your number of
victories. Those who accumulate enough victories qualify for the tournament
